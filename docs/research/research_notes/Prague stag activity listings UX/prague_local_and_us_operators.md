## Key question 1 — Prague-local operators: what they list, at what prices, with what inclusions, and how the listing/detail pages are structured

### Takeaway
The dominant Prague-local direct seller with a public price list is Prague Stag Fun (WooCommerce shop, ~83 products, EUR prices labelled "/ person", "/ group" or "/ boat", 50% deposit, "Add to cart"); the Prague venues themselves (Darling Cabaret, Goldfingers) sell stag packages per group in CZK with an "Add to cart" + "Don't forget to add" upsell row. UK agencies that resell Prague (Last Night of Freedom, Funktion Events) hide or show GBP prices and route everything through an "enquiry/quote" basket; StagDoIn's Prague catalogue is JS-rendered and could not be read.

### Inferences
- The one Prague-local operator with a transparent catalogue (Prague Stag Fun) prices **everything as a product**: per-person for anything scaling with headcount (dinners, transfers, shooting, cruises) and per-group for fixed-cost adult shows and pranks (strip show 159€/group, XXXL 224€/group, mud wrestling 499€/group, dwarf prank 306€/group). The "you share the cost!" copy on per-group items is the mechanism it uses to make a £/€ hundreds figure feel cheap.
- Local operators present add-ons as **prose lists on the product page ("Hot add-ons", "Add-on/Upgrades", "Meal upgrades") without selectors**; the only real cart mechanics are quantity + "Add to cart" and a contact form. Priced add-ons appear only when they are per-person meal/drink extras (19 EUR finger food, 29 EUR unlimited beer); adult upgrades are cross-links to separate products.
- UK resellers (LNOF, Funktion) are the ones with structured category filters incl. an explicit "X-Rated" category, "Add upgrades" blocks and enquiry baskets; the Prague venues use the e-commerce "Don't forget to add" cross-sell row.
- Minimum group sizes cluster at 8 (Prague Stag Fun), 10 (LNOF mud wrestling, alle.travel boat) and 11 per boat (Funktion beer boat); 2-person minimums exist only for tourist products (Prague Experience).

## Key question 2 — US bachelor-party companies: package + add-on structure, vocabulary, adult-entertainment wording/pricing, and photography

### Takeaway
The Las Vegas concierge model (Bachelor Vegas, Vegas VIP) sells bundled "VIP" packages at "Starting at $199 per person" or "Call for exclusive pricing", almost never uses the words "add-on/upgrade/extras", and funnels every customisation to a quote form or phone call; adult entertainment is worded as "VIP entry/seating at Sapphire/Spearmint Rhino", "stripper party bus", "strippers come to you" with no published rates and no 21+ copy. The marketplace model (Batch, ex-"The Bach") lists each experience as a card with "From $X per person" or "From $X per party", duration, rating, "Instant Book" badge, and candid guest photography.
