02 Decision

We’re shipping the quiz.

Verdict in one line

Four playful questions, group-aware weights, capped and ranked output — no swipe pile, no algorithm vendor.

01

Group signal, not initiator monopoly

The initiator no longer decides categories on behalf of everyone. Every voter answers; the group’s preferences emerge from the sum.

02

Quiz beats swipe for category selection

Eight cards collapse into four questions. The format is conversational and viral — closer to BuzzFeed than to a form.

03

One integer gives curators control

featured_weight per activity sorts the pool. A cap of 5 × days guarantees the swipe set never bloats.

03 Mechanism

How it actually works.

No machine learning. Twenty lines of code. Every decision explainable on a whiteboard.

Q

Quiz

Every voter answers 3–5 destination-specific multiple-choice questions on arrival. One question per screen, tap to advance.

GET /vote/sessions/{token}/quiz
Σ

Weighted sum

Each answer carries signed integer weights toward categories. We sum the voter’s weights and pick the three highest scoring categories.

top_K = 3
◎

Snapshot

The voter’s three categories are frozen at quiz completion. Same voter, same set — even as new voters reshape the group aggregate.

vote_session_participant_categories
→

Ranked & capped

Activities from those categories are returned sorted by featured_weight descending, capped at 5 × trip_days.

LIMIT 5 × days

05 Build vs buy

Why a recommender is the wrong tool today.

Algolia Recommend, Amazon Personalize, Recombee, Vertex AI — great products. Bad fit for this problem at this stage. Four reasons.

01 · Reality

The cold-start trap

Recommenders learn from historical interactions: clicks, bookings, dwell time. We don’t have a behavioral corpus at scale yet. They would underperform a hand-tuned weight matrix until we accumulate thousands of completed sessions.

02 · Shape

Group, not individual

Off-the-shelf systems optimise for a single user’s next click. Our problem is consensus across 5–8 people. Group recommendation is a separate research area and not what these APIs were designed to solve.

03 · Operations

Opaque, expensive, slow

External services add latency, monthly cost ($50–500+), and a black box no one on the team understands. When a user asks “why did we get suggested X?”, we wouldn’t be able to answer.

04 · Ownership

We’d outsource our taste

Trivlu’s edge is curation: featured_weight is an editorial tool a human can wield. Vendors regress toward popularity — the opposite of what a curated stag-party operator should optimise for.

Vendor by vendor

Service What it’s good at Why not for us Verdict
Amazon Personalize Enterprise CF + user-personalization recipes Designed for catalogues 100× ours. Heavy IAM + dataset setup. Costs scale with traffic. Never
Google Vertex AI Retail-grade recommendations and ranking Enterprise pricing and integration weight. Solves a different scale of problem. Never
Algolia Recommend Slots into Algolia search; collaborative filtering We don’t use Algolia search. Adding it just to recommend 72 items is over-engineered. Never
Recombee REST-first recommender, free tier, group endpoints Best of the bunch for our shape. Still solves a problem we don’t have at current scale. Far future
pgvector + embeddings Semantic similarity in our existing Postgres Useful when admin curation becomes a bottleneck. Cheap to layer in later as a ranking signal. Next phase

When this changes

The day we have a thousand completed sessions, a real cold-start corpus, and admin curation can’t keep up with destination growth — that’s when we revisit. Until then, every line of recommendation code is a line we can read, test, and explain.

06 Roadmap

From decision to signal.

v1 · This sprint

Quiz + featured_weight + cap

Ship the spec at docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-11-quiz-driven-voting-design.md. Seed Prague quiz on launch.

v1.5 · Weeks 2–4

Observe & tune

Track quiz completion, swipe abandonment, archetype distribution. Iterate question wording and weights.

v2 · Q3 maybe

pgvector for semantic ranking

Embed activity descriptions. Use them as a tiebreaker for featured_weight when curation lags behind catalogue growth.

v3 · When data demands

Revisit hosted recommender

If session count crosses 10k and curation can’t keep up, evaluate Recombee as an additional signal — not a replacement.
